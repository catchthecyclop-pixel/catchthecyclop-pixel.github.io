Privacy Policy

Effective 4 October 2026

In short: there are no accounts, and we do not collect, store or sell personal information. Your progress stays on your device. The game has no ads, no analytics and no purchases, and it sends nothing anywhere.

1. Who we are

Catch the Cyclop is made by Big Eye Studio. Contact: catchthecyclop@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your device

To save your game, the following is kept only on your device, in the app's local storage: your progress through the 12 worlds and missions, your gold bars and gems, the runners and weapons you have unlocked and equipped, the treasure-chest tally, and your settings (sound, music, reduced flashing, tutorial seen). None of it identifies you and none of it is sent to us. Erase it at any time with Settings, About & legal, Reset progress, or by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.

To choose the right graphics quality, the game reads the name of your device's graphics chip. It is used on the device at that moment and is never stored or sent.

3. No advertising

The game shows no ads and contains no advertising code, so no advertising ID or other device data is collected or shared with anyone.

4. Nothing else

The game contains no analytics, tracking pixels, social-media plugins or other third-party code, and it does not use cookies. This website uses no cookies or trackers either.

5. Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the Israeli Privacy Protection Law, the EU and UK GDPR and US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, to object to or restrict its use, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data-protection authority (in Israel, the Privacy Protection Authority). You are not obliged to provide any information to play. Since we hold no personal information about you, Reset progress (or clearing the app's data) covers all of your data.

7. Security and retention

We keep nothing about you on our side. Data on your device remains until you erase it.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here, in the game and on its store page with a new effective date.
